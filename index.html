THE ARTIST

The Sound of Antos Jr

Emerging from the rapidly evolving hip-hop renaissance of Kerala, India, Antos Jr stands out as one of the underground's most dynamic lyricists and vocal forces.

From recording early verses in home studios to earning thousands of monthly listeners across global platforms, his sonic identity merges the rhythmic complexity of Malayalam and South Indian slang with international drill and trap cadences.

Whether trading intense bars on "100 Miles" with ShellShock, riding vibrant rhythms on "Cult Papi" alongside Dcypher, or delivering introspective narrative arcs on "FROM MY 16" and his 2026 smash "Rich Kanavukal", Antos Jr crafts music tailored for speaker-rattling car stereos and high-voltage stage cyphers alike.
